Ring Studio Help

Stream — Connect in Chrome, then start the raw stream. Reboot restarts the connected ring. Use Connect again after it restarts. Click a metric to request a reading. Solid traces are raw, dashed are smoothed and dotted are interpolated. The rate next to Accelerometer counts incoming packets and changes in XYZ separately. Stop the stream before toggling Ring gestures to compare it with the raw-only stream.

Mapping — Set the MIDI output and channel. Motion input ranges map to MIDI 0–127. To teach a gesture, add a name and record at least two examples with the ring in the same orientation. Perform each gesture once during the recording window, returning to rest between examples. Enable recognition and assign a note or CC.

Firmware — Connect your ring, choose its firmware from the dropdown, then confirm Flash firmware. Keep the ring charged and nearby until it restarts. Reconnect to check the running version. Experimental images may prevent normal operation.

Log & commands — Settings sends a checksummed packet of up to 15 bytes. Framed sends one command byte with optional data. Use loads a catalog command into the editor.
